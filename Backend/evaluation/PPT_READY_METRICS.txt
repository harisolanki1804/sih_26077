[METRIC 1] WARNING LEAD TIME
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Traditional (IMD):
    - Alerts issued AFTER thresholds are crossed (reactive)

    - Alerts issued BEFORE conditions worsen (predictive)

[METRIC 2] FALSE ALARM REDUCTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Traditional:  30.5% false alarm rate (18 false alarms / 31 total alerts)
  VARUNA:       27.1% false alarm rate (16 false alarms / 29 total alerts)
  IMPROVEMENT:  3.4% fewer false alarms

  Why fewer false alarms:
    1. Physics-informed model enforces conservation laws
    2. Multi-hazard decomposition (3 hazard types, not one)
    3. Cross-source fusion reduces single-source noise
    4. Conformal prediction flags uncertain predictions

[METRIC 6] HYPERLOCAL GRANULARITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Traditional:  City-wide single alert (e.g., 'Mumbai: heavy rain warning')
  VARUNA:       90 individual cells, each with:
    - Risk score (0-100)
    - Flood depth (cm)
    - Named locality (Andheri West, Juhu, Bandra, etc.)
    - 3-hazard probabilities (thunderstorm, cloudburst, flash flood)
    - Top 3 risk drivers (XAI explanation)
    - Confidence interval (conformal prediction)

[METRIC 7] FORECAST CONFIDENCE (Conformal Prediction)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Traditional:  Binary alert with no confidence measure
  VARUNA:        Statistically guaranteed 90% coverage interval
    - Risk scores come with +-X% confidence bounds
    - Anomaly detection flags unusual patterns
    - Similar historical case lookup (e.g., 2005 Mumbai deluge)
    - Trust score: 0-100 based on 4-component calibration
